## 2025-02-12 - [Floating Contact Button ARIA]\n**Learning:** Expanding/collapsing toggle menus need 'aria-expanded' and 'aria-controls' to properly communicate their state and relationship to the screen reader. It's safe to use conditional rendering for 'aria-controls' in React.\n**Action:** Add aria-expanded to any toggle button and aria-controls to link to the expanded content id.
